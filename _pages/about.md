I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present). My PhD supervisor is Professor Junxian He.

I completed my B.Eng. (2020-2024) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), graduating in June 2024. During my undergraduate studies at SJTU, I was previously advised by Professor Junxian He and received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

Research Focus & Interests
======
My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include:
* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM truthfulness and Interpretability
