I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). Prior to joining HKUST, I received my B.Eng. from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024, where I was honored with the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship and also conducted research under the guidance of Prof. Junxian He.

My research broadly focuses on **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** and **Machine Learning (ML)**. Specifically, I am interested in:
- **LLM Reasoning & Reinforcement Learning**: Enhancing reasoning capabilities and verifiable problem-solving in large language models.
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)**: Understanding and mitigating perceptual bottlenecks and hallucinations in multi-modal models.
- **LLM Truthfulness & Interpretability**: Analyzing internal representations and mechanisms governing model truthfulness and reliability.
