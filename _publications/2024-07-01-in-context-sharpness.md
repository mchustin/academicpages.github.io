Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, **Junteng Liu**, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He

### Abstract / Overview
Hallucination mitigation in large language models requires early detection mechanisms during text generation. We present an inner representation perspective on model uncertainty, introducing the concept of *In-Context Sharpness*. We show that fluctuations and sharpness signatures in hidden layer representations act as proactive alerts for impending hallucinations, allowing effective decoding-time intervention and mitigation.
