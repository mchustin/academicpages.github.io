### Authors
Jinghan Zhang, Shiqi Chen, **Junteng Liu**, Junxian He

### Abstract / Overview
Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) methods (such as adapters and LoRA) enable modular adaptation of foundation models to specific tasks. In this paper, we explore the composability of these modules using elementary arithmetic operations (such as addition, scaling, and subtraction) directly in parameter space. We demonstrate that arithmetic composition allows models to merge multi-task capabilities, eliminate unwanted behaviors, and transfer skills without requiring joint retraining.
