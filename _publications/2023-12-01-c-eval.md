### Abstract / Overview
**C-Eval** is the first comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite designed to assess the advanced knowledge and reasoning abilities of foundation models. It consists of 13,948 multi-choice questions spanning 52 diverse disciplines across four difficulty levels (middle school, high school, college, and professional). C-Eval provides a standardized, rigorous benchmark for understanding model strengths and limitations in non-English linguistic and cultural contexts.
