### Abstract / Overview
Advanced mathematical and logical reasoning remains one of the core challenges in large language models (LLMs). This work introduces **SynLogic**, a framework designed to synthesize large-scale, verifiable reasoning data with rigorous logical structure. By generating complex problem spaces paired with automatic verification pipelines, SynLogic empowers foundation models to learn robust multi-step logical deduction, generalization, and verifiable problem solving.
