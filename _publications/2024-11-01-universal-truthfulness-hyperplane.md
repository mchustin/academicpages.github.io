### Authors
**Junteng Liu** (First Author), Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He

### Abstract / Overview
Ensuring that large language models produce truthful statements rather than hallucinations or falsehoods is critical for their safe deployment. In this work, we investigate the internal geometry of representations across various state-of-the-art LLMs and discover the existence of a **universal truthfulness hyperplane**. This hyperplane linearly separates truthful internal representations from untruthful ones across diverse topics and datasets, demonstrating that truthfulness is encoded in a structured and identifiable manner within the model's activation space.
