### Abstract / Overview
Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have shown remarkable capabilities across diverse multimodal benchmarks, yet they frequently encounter severe bottlenecks when interpreting complex charts and graphical data. In this paper, we systematically dissect the perception bottleneck in VLMs for chart understanding, identifying where visual encoders fail to resolve fine-grained graphical elements and how these perceptual failures propagate into reasoning errors. We propose targeted evaluation methodologies and architectures to alleviate this bottleneck.
